Guild Leader: [[Shadow Claw Leader]]

Longtime Rivals with [[The Ivory Lions]].

The number 2 Guild for quite some time, they are known for always pushing the envelope. Their philosophy is that the "Ends Justify the Means" and will do whatever it takes to keep the Kingdom safe.

Rumor says that they use Forbidden Magic and deals with horrific entities, and that the Crown turns a blind eye because the Kingdom relies heavily upon them.

They kill the bad guys, but they steal their methods.
